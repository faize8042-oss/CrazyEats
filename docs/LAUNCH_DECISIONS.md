# Owner decisions required for public launch

This worksheet is not a published policy or legal document. Do not publish placeholder business details.

| Decision | Needed information |
| --- | --- |
| Operator | Registered/trading name, contact address, support email and phone |
| Service | Launch city/areas, delivery radius or postcodes, courier responsibility, manual dispatch process |
| Charges | Menu ownership, delivery charges, platform commission and applicable tax handling |
| Cancellations | Customer/restaurant cancellation cutoff, failed-delivery handling, complaint contact |
| Refunds | Eligible situations, approval responsibility, refund route/timing after a provider is selected |
| Privacy | Data contact, retention periods, service-provider list and deletion/request process |
| Sales | Restaurant onboarding agreement, service pricing, support hours and maintenance scope |
| Infrastructure | Domain, hosting region/account, mail sender, backup storage and monitoring owner |
| Payments | Pakistan merchant provider, settlement account, supported methods and test/live onboarding |

Before launch, convert these approved choices into operator-specific privacy, terms and support pages, have them reviewed as appropriate, and link them from the site. Credentials belong in private environment configuration, not this file or chat.
